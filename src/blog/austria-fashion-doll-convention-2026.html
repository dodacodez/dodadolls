Meet me at the Austria Fashion Doll Convention 2026

November 2026 - I will be present & selling at the Austria Fashion Doll Convention in Vienna. After a great first convention in September, we're back just in time for Christmas!

12.12.2026, 2-6pm
VHS Floridsdorf
Angerer Strasse 14
1210 Vienna
Entry: 5 EUR, kids free

## What awaits You It is the best time of the year for some Barbie joy! I cannot wait to surprise you with: - Never before seen or listed dolls - Christmas OOAKs - Rare Black Label dolls - Dolls from the Barbie Basics metallic line - My custom jewelry for the human collector: pendants and hair clips showing off busts of Steffie and vintage Barbie, as well as illustrated portraits of Superstar Barbie and Tarina Tarantino Barbie ...and so much more! For larger purchases, I also accept digital payment on the spot.

## Good to know Overall, there will be around 13 exhibitors from all over Austria. The location is easily reachable by public transport: Bahnhof Floridsdorf (S+U), tram stop "Am Spitz" (trams 25, 26, 30, 31 / buses 20A, 33A) or "Floridsdorfer Markt". Coming by car? Since it is a Saturday, there is free parking available. ## More info Join the convention's Facebook group for updates. Looking for something else or want to connect directly? Check my Dollfinder.
